Ways to add your clothes

Four ways in — pick whichever matches the pile in front of you.

One piece at a time

Photograph a piece, add it, done. Best for new buys and one-offs — you confirm its name and details as you go.

A whole pile at once

Choose many photos at once and each becomes its own piece, named and tagged automatically in the background. Review them in your wardrobe afterwards. One photo per piece — extra angles can be added later from the piece itself.

From Google Photos

On Android, Google Photos quietly builds a wardrobe collection from photos of you — clean cutouts of your pieces, ready-made. Open Google Photos, find the collection, save the pieces you want to your camera roll, then add them here like any photo. Closet doesn't connect to Google Photos directly — it's a save-then-add path, and the pieces arrive looking clean.

From your clipboard

Seeing it online? Copy the product photo — right-click it and choose Copy image — or take a screenshot, then paste it straight in: Ctrl+V on Windows, Cmd+V on a Mac. Works on the Add pieces screen, in a shop check, or anywhere in the app — we'll ask whether it's a new piece or a check. No saving files first. On a phone, the same screens carry a Paste a photo button — on Android it reads the clipboard directly; on iPhone the browser keeps the clipboard private, so save the photo and upload it instead.
